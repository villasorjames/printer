How it is derived

The KNO3 is shared between two reactions:

  • 5 Mg + 2 KNO3 → 5 MgO + K2O + N2 (0.4 mol KNO3 per Mg)
  • 10 Al + 6 KNO3 → 5 Al2O3 + 3 K2O + 3 N2 (0.6 mol KNO3 per Al)

With equal moles of Mg and Al, KNO3 needed = 0.4 + 0.6 = 1 mol per mol of each metal, so Mg : Al : KNO3 = 1 : 1 : 1.
